How It Secures Your Drive

A seamless 4-step automated process.

1

Pre-Scan & Backup

Validates the drive and safely copies existing files to a temporary folder on your Desktop, excluding known virus extensions.

2

Format & Structure

Performs a fast NTFS format to enable Access Control Lists (ACLs), then creates a designated 'My_Files' safe zone.

3

Permission Lock

Applies a strict Deny-Write rule to the root drive preventing shortcut generation, while granting full access to the safe zone.

4

Restore & Cleanup

Moves your backed-up data back into the protected 'My_Files' folder and permanently deletes the temporary desktop backup.

Documentation

Everything you need to know before running USB Shield.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 Operating System.
  • Administrator Privileges (required for disk formatting and ACL modifications).
  • Enough free space on your C: Drive (Desktop) to hold the temporary backup of your USB data.

How to Use

USB Shield is a portable executable. No installation is required.

  1. Download the usb_shield.exe file from the release section.
  2. Plug in your USB Flash Drive.
  3. Run the executable. Grant Administrator permissions if prompted by Windows UAC.
  4. Select your Drive Letter from the dropdown menu and ensure "Auto Backup" is checked.
  5. Click Format & Protect USB and wait for the progress bar to complete.

Important Warning

If you disable the Auto Backup feature, your USB drive will be formatted immediately, resulting in permanent data loss. Only disable this if you are securing an empty drive.
